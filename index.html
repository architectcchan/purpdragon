Cultivation

WE HONOR THE WHOLE PLANT
THROUGHOUT THE PROCESS

From seed to shelf, every decision protects potency, terpenes, and the integrity of the smoke.

01

HYDRO-SOIL MEDIA

Premium hydroponic soil blend combining the precision of hydro with the natural buffering power of soil for terpene-rich flower production.

02

FULL-SPECTRUM LIGHT

Dedicated veg rooms with full-spectrum LEDs. Flowering rooms with LED + HPS to fuel resin production, trichome density, and terpene expression.

03

14-DAY SLOW CURE

After harvest, flower is hung on the stock and slow-cured in humidity-controlled rooms preserving terpene profile, potency, and smoothness.

04

HAND-TRIMMED TOPS

Carefully sorted to remove small buds, finished with a precise hand trim. Every batch is lab-tested before it's sealed and shipped.
